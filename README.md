# Airbnb-Performance-Dashboard

Airbnb Performance Dashboard

A Power BI data analytics project that analyzes Airbnb listings,
reviews, pricing, ratings, host trust, market share, growth trends, and
seasonality across major global cities.

📌 Project Overview

This project uses two primary datasets:

listings.csv --- Airbnb property/listing-level information

reviews.csv --- Airbnb customer review activity

The data is transformed and modeled in Power BI to create an interactive
Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard.

The dashboard contains three major analytical areas:

Growth --- Airbnb listing growth and market development over
time

Ratings --- City-level ratings, pricing, listings, reviews, and
host performance

Seasonality --- Review frequency, monthly trends, reviewer
behavior, and host identity/trust

The project dataset represented in the dashboard contains approximately
279,712 listings, 182,024 hosts, 144 property types, 10
cities, and 5.373 million reviews.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze Airbnb's listing growth over time.

Compare Airbnb performance across major global cities.

Identify cities with the highest listing and review volumes.

Compare average prices across different property types.

Analyze detailed customer ratings.

Understand review frequency and customer behavior.

Identify seasonal patterns in Airbnb reviews.

Analyze host verification and trust signals.

Present business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

🔎 Key Insights

Based on the completed dashboard:

Paris, New York, and Sydney account for almost half of the total
listings and approximately 59% of total reviews.

Paris has the highest number of listings and reviews among the
analyzed cities.

Hotel rooms show a substantially higher average price than other
room/property categories.

Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro are among the best-rated cities
overall, while Hong Kong and Istanbul have comparatively lower
overall ratings.

Cleanliness and value for money are generally among the weaker
rating dimensions.

Most customers leave reviews only a small number of times;
approximately 98.8% of customers reviewed three times or fewer.

Paris and Rome have a strong share of reviews from April to
August, reflecting the European summer travel period.

New York shows increased review activity during November and
December, consistent with the holiday season.

More than two-thirds of Airbnb hosts are fully verified, while
nearly all hosts provide at least one trust signal.

🚀 How to Use

Download or clone this repository.

Place listings.csv and reviews.csv inside the data folder.

Open the Power BI .pbix file.

If required, update the data-source paths in Power Query.

Refresh the dataset.

Explore the Growth, Ratings, and Seasonality pages.

Use slicers and interactive visuals to analyze individual cities and
categories.


The project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow from raw CSV
datasets to an interactive business intelligence dashboard.
